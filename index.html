Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is GetPYQ?

GetPYQ is an open digital library of previous year examination question papers (PYQs) and solutions dedicated to BPUT engineering and technology students.

How to download BPUT PYQs for free?

Filter by your branch and semester or use the search box to find your subject. You can view the question paper PDF directly in your browser or download it free for offline preparation.

GetPYQ is an independent open academic archive for BPUT engineering students. Not affiliated with Biju Patnaik University of Technology.
